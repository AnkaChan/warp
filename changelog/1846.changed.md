Speed up BVH and mesh AABB and sphere queries by testing both children at the parent, carrying the near child in registers, and pushing the far child onto the traversal stack as a memory-free tagged payload pair (bounded by a tree-depth budget so results are never dropped). AABB queries run 20-29% faster and sphere queries 17-31% faster on an L40; query results are unchanged. See ``design/bvh-payload-pair-traversal.md``.
